# Turkish

## Scope

Project, publication, genre, and house rules take precedence. Otherwise, use current Turkey Turkish appropriate to the
audience and domain.

## Authority

- Preserve meaning, facts, evidence, certainty, attribution, citations, terminology, protected material, and required
  structure unless the task explicitly changes them.
- Derive expression from the purpose, evidence, audience, genre, author, and context. Preserve supplied voice,
  defensible cadence, deliberate repetition, and functional formality.
- For broad revision, work from purpose and structure through paragraphs, sentences, and wording. Keep a focused edit
  local when those larger surfaces are not in scope.
- Never invent specificity, examples, anecdotes, quotations, reactions, authority, or evidence to strengthen the prose.
- Re-read changed prose for lost obligations, unsupported additions, changed claim strength, broken logic, unstable
  terminology, flattened voice, and format damage.

## Grammar and information structure

- Let information structure govern word order. Turkish often places focus near the predicate, but emphasis, contrast,
  rhythm, and genre can justify another order.
- Keep modifiers, relative clauses, and qualifiers close enough to their heads to prevent attachment ambiguity. Keep
  case, possessive, and compound-noun relations recoverable.
- Check person, agreement, tense, aspect, mood, evidentiality, negation, and scope after each material rearrangement.
- Give pronouns, demonstratives, ellipsis, and omitted subjects clear referents while preserving deliberate omission or
  repetition when the relation remains clear.

## Expression and terminology

- Write Turkish relations instead of translating source word order. Prefer direct actors and verbs when they improve
  comprehension. Keep passive and nominalized forms when the genre makes them functional.
- Prefer established project and domain terminology. Translate the contextual sense, and repeat one stable term when it
  denotes one concept.
- Remove English-shaped padding, bureaucratic euphemism, and unsupported inflation only when meaning, evidential stance,
  register, and deliberate cadence remain intact.
- Treat spelling, punctuation, capitalization, headings, numerals, abbreviations, citations, semicolons, dashes, and
  parenthetical style as contextual publication decisions.

## Scholarly prose

Preserve claim strength, attribution, evidential distance, defined terms, citations, and justified passives or
nominalizations. Do not turn a source-qualified claim into the narrator's assertion.

## Translation

Translate intended function rather than visible word order. Preserve meaningful ambiguity, register, force, emphasis,
terminology, tense, modality, evidential source, and negation scope. Resolve a missing distinction only when context
supports the choice.
